Writing competition · Due Thursday, Oct. 29

My Natural High / Everyday Hero

Write 200–300 words answering: What gives you a natural high, and who is an everyday hero in your life who helps you make healthy choices?

1 · Your natural high

Name it. Give a specific example of how it makes you feel and why it matters.

2 · Your everyday hero

A real person (family, coach, teacher, friend, or mentor) and one specific thing they did or said.

3 · Your hero choice

End with one choice you'll make to stay drug free and make a difference for someone else.

Need a start?

"My natural high is ___ because when I ___, I feel ___. My everyday hero is ___ because they ___."

Use any class
  • ELA: a strong opening, personal details, and editing.
  • Science / Health: add a short paragraph on how a natural high affects your brain and body differently from drugs or vaping.
  • Social Studies: interview your hero with 3 questions, quote one answer, and connect their story to your community.
How to turn it in

Name, grade, and homeroom teacher at the top. Typed or neatly handwritten. Give it to your homeroom or ELA teacher by the end of the day Thursday.

Prizes

One winner per grade gets a certificate, a hallway display, and a shout-out on the morning announcements. Every complete entry counts for your homeroom!

How essays are judged (out of 12)
  • Clear message: your natural high and drug-free choice are clear from start to finish.
  • Personal example: specific, vivid examples for both your natural high and your hero.
  • Hero choice: a concrete, believable choice you'll actually make.
  • Length & clarity: 200–300 words, easy to read, few errors.
